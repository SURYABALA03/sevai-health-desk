What happens after you send it

  1. Instant reply. You get a reference number and an automatic message with next steps.
  2. Sorted by urgency. Our triage assistant looks for warning signs like chest pain or breathing trouble, so urgent requests are read first.
  3. A volunteer calls you. Usually within 24 hours, and within 2 hours for urgent requests.
